# Head of Department (HOD) User Guide

## Academic Bulletin Review & Management System (ABRMS)

---

## 1. Logging In

1. Open your web browser and navigate to the ABRMS platform URL provided by your institution.
2. You will see the **Sign In** page.
3. Enter your **university email address** and **password** in the respective fields.
4. Click **"Sign In"**.
5. Upon successful login, you will be redirected to the **Dashboard**.

> **Note:** Your account will have been created by a Super Admin. If you do not have credentials, please contact the Academic Planning Office.

---

## 2. Understanding the Dashboard

Once logged in, you will see the Dashboard which provides an overview of your department's bulletin activity:

- **Total Submissions** — Number of bulletin submissions across your programmes.
- **In Review** — Submissions currently awaiting action from reviewers.
- **Approved** — Submissions that have cleared all review phases.
- **Open Comments** — Unresolved feedback from reviewers that may need your attention.

### Quick Actions

- **"New Submission"** button (top-right) — Start a new bulletin submission.
- **Organisation Overview** — Quick links to Schools, Departments, Programmes, Users, and Master Courses.

---

## 3. Creating a New Submission

1. From the Dashboard, click the **"New Submission"** button, or navigate to **Submissions → New Submission**.
2. On the New Submission page:
   - **Filter by School** (optional): Narrow down the programme list.
   - **Select Programme**: Choose the programme you are creating the bulletin for. As an HOD, you will only see programmes within your department.
   - **Submission Title**: This is auto-generated (e.g., _"B.Sc. Software Engineering - Academic Bulletin"_) but can be manually edited if needed.
3. Click **"Create Submission"**.
4. You will be redirected to the **Submission Editor** where you can begin filling in each section.

> **Important:** A submission can only be created when an active **Review Cycle** exists. If you see a message saying "No Active Review Cycle," contact the Academic Planning Office.

---

## 4. Editing a Submission (The Submission Editor)

The Submission Editor is the core workspace where you populate all sections of the bulletin. The left sidebar shows all available sections. Click on any section to start editing it.

### Section Tabs (in order):

#### 4.1 Deans & HODs

- Provide the **Dean Image URL** — a direct link to the Dean's profile picture.
- Provide the **HOD Image URL** — a direct link to the HOD's profile picture.
- You can paste a **Google Drive** share link and it will be automatically converted to a displayable image format.
- A preview of each image will appear below the input field once a valid URL is entered.

#### 4.2 Staff Directory

- Add all **Academic Staff**, **Adjunct Academic Staff**, and **Non-Academic Staff** for the department.
- For each staff member, you will provide: Name, Title, Academic Rank, Highest Qualification, Area of Specialisation, and Email.

#### 4.3 Programme Overview

- **Philosophy** — Write the programme's philosophy (must be 150–250 words). This section is also checked for **GRACE compliance** (Global relevance, Research focus, Adventist rooted, Competent community, Entrepreneurship).
- **Mission & Vision** — Provide the programme's mission and vision statements.
- **Objectives** — List the specific objectives of the programme.
- **Unique Features** — Highlight any distinguishing characteristics.
- **Employability Skills** — Describe skills graduates will acquire.

#### 4.4 Admission Requirements

- **UTME Entry Requirements** — O'Level subject requirements and minimum scores.
- **Direct Entry Requirements** — Requirements for direct entry candidates.
- **Inter-University Transfer Requirements** — Requirements for transfer candidates.

#### 4.5 Graduation Requirements

- **Graduation Requirements** — Describe what students must accomplish to graduate.
- **Minimum CGPA** — Set the minimum cumulative grade point average.
- **Total Credit Units** — Specify the total credit units required.
- **Programme Duration** — Select the minimum programme duration (4–6 years).

#### 4.6 Course Structure

- Add courses organised by **Level** (100–500) and **Semester** (First/Second).
- For each course, specify: Course Code, Title, Credit Units, Status (Core/Elective), and Prerequisites.

#### 4.7 Course Details

- For each course added in the Course Structure, provide:
  - **Learning Outcomes**
  - **Course Content**
  - **Lab/Practical Work** (if applicable)
  - **Entrepreneurial/Practical Aspect** (if applicable)
- Each course description is also checked for **GRACE compliance**.

#### 4.8 Regulations & Changes

- **Programme Regulations** — Any regulations governing the programme.
- **Summary of Changes** — Document any changes made since the last bulletin cycle.

### Auto-Save

Your work is **automatically saved every 3 seconds** after you stop typing. You will see a save indicator showing the status:

- _Saving..._ — Changes are being saved.
- _Saved_ — All changes have been saved successfully.
- _Error_ — Save failed; try again or check your connection.

You can also manually save at any time by clicking the **"Save Draft"** button.

---

## 5. Submitting for Review

Once all sections are complete and validated:

1. Click the **"Submit for Review"** button at the top of the Submission Editor.
2. The system will run validation checks. You may see errors if:
   - The Philosophy is not within the 150–250 word limit.
   - The Programme Duration has not been selected.
   - The Philosophy has not passed GRACE compliance.
   - Any course descriptions have failed GRACE compliance.
3. If all validations pass, the submission moves to **Phase 2: Departmental Review** and its status changes from "Draft" to "In Review."

> **Only HODs and Super Admins can officially submit** a bulletin for review. Programme Coordinators can edit drafts, but cannot submit them.

---

## 6. Tracking Submission Progress

Navigate to **Submissions** from the sidebar to see a list of all your submissions.

Each submission displays:

- **Programme Name** — The programme the submission belongs to.
- **Review Cycle** — Which cycle it belongs to.
- **Phase** — The current review phase (Phases 1–11).
- **Status** — Draft, In Review, Approved, or Rejected.
- **Comments** — Number of reviewer comments.
- **Last Updated** — When it was last modified.

### Review Phases

| Phase | Name                     | Description                              |
| ----- | ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- |
| 1     | Preparation              | Initial draft creation by the department |
| 2     | Departmental Review      | Internal departmental review             |
| 3     | School Approval          | Dean-level approval                      |
| 4     | Academic Planning Review | Central academic planning review         |
| 5     | Corrections              | Corrections based on review feedback     |
| 6     | Final Consolidation      | Final consolidation of all changes       |
| 7     | Senate Approval          | University Senate approval               |
| 8     | Editing                  | Professional editing                     |
| 9     | Typesetting              | Layout and typesetting                   |
| 10    | Distribution             | Distribution preparation                 |
| 11    | Post-Release             | Post-publication tracking                |

---

## 7. Responding to Comments

Reviewers may leave feedback on your submission. To view and respond:

1. Open the submission by clicking **"View"** in the Submissions list.
2. Scroll to the **Comments** section at the bottom of the editor.
3. Comments are tagged by type:
   - **Suggestion** (blue) — Non-blocking recommendations.
   - **Issue** (red) — Problems that must be addressed.
   - **Question** (yellow) — Clarifications needed.
   - **Approval** (green) — Positive feedback.
4. Address any outstanding issues by editing the relevant section.
5. You can add your own comments to communicate with reviewers.

---

## 8. Previewing & Exporting the Bulletin

At any time, you can preview how the final bulletin will look:

1. While in the Submission Editor, click the **"Preview"** tab.
2. The preview shows the bulletin in its final layout order:
   - Deans & HODs Profile → Staff Directory → Philosophy & Objectives → Admission Requirements → Graduation Requirements → Course Structure → Course Descriptions → Regulations & Changes.

### Exporting

- Navigate to **Bulletins** from the sidebar (if available to your role).
- Select the programme and export as **Word (.docx)** or **PDF**.
- The exported document will follow the exact same section ordering as the preview.
